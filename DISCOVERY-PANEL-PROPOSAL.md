### 1. **YouTube Search** (EXISTS - DO NOT TOUCH)

- Works on all 18 pages
- No changes whatsoever

### 2. **Musicians Tab** (NEW - ADDED BELOW SEARCH)
- Clickable cards for each band member
- Cross-references to other pages in the book
- Mini-biographies
- Links to search for each musician's work

### 3. **Film Career Tab** (NEW - UNIQUE TO DEXTER GORDON)
- Highlights his role in "Round Midnight"
- Embedded YouTube clip option
- Historical context about jazz musicians in film
- Academy Award nomination details

### 4. **Album Tracks Tab** (NEW)
- Full track listing with times
- "Search for this track" button for each song
- Composer information for standards
- "Play Full Album" playlist builder
- Educational notes (key, tempo, style)

### 5. **Connections Tab** (NEW)
- Smart navigation to other pages
- Visual similarity connections (Reid Miles designs)
- Era-based recommendations
- Personnel cross-references

## 🎨 Design Principles

1. **Search is Sacred**: Never hide, remove, or diminish the search functionality
2. **Tabs, Not Replacements**: Discovery features appear in collapsible tabs below search
3. **Internal Navigation**: All links point to other pages in THIS book
4. **Progressive Disclosure**: Start simple, expand on click
5. **Visual Hierarchy**: Search remains prominent at top
